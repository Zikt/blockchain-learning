# Recording an explainer video

Videos are optional. When you have time, record a 5-minute version of a post.

**Where to put it:** upload to YouTube (unlisted is fine) or Loom, then paste the link into the `video:` line at the top of the post. Don't commit video files to this repo; they're large and GitHub isn't built for them (`.gitignore` already blocks common video formats).

**A 5-minute structure**

| Time | Section |
|------|---------|
| 0:00–0:30 | The question, and why it matters |
| 0:30–2:30 | The idea, drawn on a whiteboard, slide or paper |
| 2:30–4:00 | Demo: run your code from that week's folder |
| 4:00–5:00 | What surprised you, and what's next |

**Teach to learn:** record once without notes. Wherever you stumble or say "basically", that's a gap. Note it in LEARNING_LOG.md and go back to it.

**Kit:** a phone or laptop camera, OBS Studio (free) for screen recording, and a quiet room.

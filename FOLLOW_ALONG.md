# Follow along: do this course yourself

This repo is a 12-week, 10-hours-a-week path from "what is a hash?" to a deployed four-part blockchain system (the trust stack). You can take the same path.

## 1. Get your own copy

- Click **Use this template** on GitHub (or fork), and name it `blockchain-learning`.
- Your copy keeps the plan, templates, scripts and tests, but it's your repo and your commit history, which becomes your own proof of learning.

## 2. Reset the progress to zero

The author's ticks, logs and posts are in this repo as examples. Before you start:

- Untick every box in `PLAN.md` and the week READMEs (or run `python scripts/apply_tracker.py --reset` if your copy of the script supports it).
- Clear `LEARNING_LOG.md` and `OUTPUTS.md` links, and delete `blog/` posts you didn't write.
- Run `python scripts/set_github_user.py <your-username>` so badges and links point at your repo.

## 3. Open the tracker

`tracker/index.html` is the same tracker the author used. Open it in a browser, or enable GitHub Pages and open `https://<you>.github.io/blockchain-learning/tracker/`. Set your start date at the top: every due date and the countdown shift to match. Your progress is saved in your browser, so use the same browser each time, and paste your repo's `progress.json` into **Repo sync** to keep them in line.

## 4. Follow the rhythm

`PLAN.md` lists every task with its resources and due date. Each week:

- **Tue:** reading and videos · **Thu:** reading, then start the build · **Fri:** build · **Sat:** finish, write the log, push
- Commit every session. On Saturday, fill in the week README and push.
- Miss a week? Skip ahead rather than doubling up, and move your start date.

## 5. Stay safe

Testnets only. Never commit private keys, seed phrases or `.env` files, and use a separate browser profile for your wallet.
